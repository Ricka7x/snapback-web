Cookie Policy

Last updated October 28, 2026

This page covers cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage) on snapbackapp.com. The Snapback app doesn't use cookies; what it sends is described in the Privacy Policy. By default, nothing for analytics loads until you accept.

Always on

  • Your cookie choice (snapback-consent, local storage): remembers whether you accepted or declined, so we don't ask on every page. Kept until you clear it.

Only if you accept

  • Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC): cookies such as _ga and _ga_*, kept up to 2 years. Counts visits and pages viewed and where visitors come from. Data is processed by Google, possibly outside your country. Google's privacy policy.
  • Microsoft Clarity (Microsoft): cookies such as _clck and _clsk, kept up to 1 year. Records anonymised clicks, scrolling and heatmaps, and session replays, so we can see where the site is confusing. Microsoft's privacy statement.

We don't use advertising cookies and we don't share this data with advertisers. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as a decline.

Change your choice

You can accept or decline again whenever you like: . Declining after accepting removes these cookies and reloads the page. You can also delete cookies in your browser settings.
